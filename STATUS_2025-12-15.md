## Alert Behavior

### Missing Job Alerts
- **No cooldown** - alerts every cycle (15-30 min)
- **Purpose**: Ensures critical failures aren't missed
- **Management**: Acknowledge deleted jobs with `~/will/anomaly ack <job_name>`

### Duration Anomaly Alerts
- **30-minute cooldown** - prevents spam
- **Threshold**: >2.5σ from mean duration
- **Auto-resolves**: When job duration returns to normal

## Managing Job Deletions

When you permanently remove a job from crontab:
1. Detector will alert every cycle
2. You'll get ntfy notifications
3. Acknowledge the deletion: `~/will/anomaly ack <job_name>`
4. Job removed from monitoring, alerts stop
